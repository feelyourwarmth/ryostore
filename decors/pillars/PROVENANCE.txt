Pillars of Creation - Ryoku decor

content/source.png is the raw public-domain image, normalised to PNG at up to 1800px on the long edge. content/dither.png is the Ryoku one-bit bone-on-transparent variant baked with bin/art/ryodither. assets/preview.webp is catalogue media (the dithered look on a dark ground) and is not installed with the runtime payload.
